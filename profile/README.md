## About

Havoc Security Labs is an offensive security consultancy founded in 2026 and based in Louisville, Kentucky. We help teams find and fix real weaknesses in their applications, infrastructure, and AI systems through hands-on, attacker-minded testing and clear, actionable reporting.

## What we do

- **Penetration testing**: web applications, APIs, and networks, tested against your scope and rules of engagement.
- **AI / LLM security**: red teaming of LLM applications, agents, RAG pipelines, and tool-using systems.
- **Smart contract security**: audits with reproducible proof-of-concept exploits.
- **Bug bounty research**: coordinated, in-scope vulnerability research and responsible disclosure.
- **Security tooling**: custom tooling and automation that supports our offensive work.

## How we work

- **Scoped and authorized**: every engagement is covered by a written agreement and tested only within agreed scope.
- **Evidence-based**: findings come with reproducible steps, real impact, and practical remediation guidance, not scanner noise.
- **Clear reporting**: reports written for both engineers and decision-makers.
- **Responsible disclosure**: vulnerabilities are reported privately and handled with care.

## Open source
